Got questions about passes, workshops, or speakers? We’ve got answers.

What is the difference between the $399 Standard Pass and $749 Pro Pass? +
The Standard Pass ($399) gives you access to all 3 conference days, 2 workshop sessions, and 30-day recording access. Upgrading to the Pro Pass ($749) unlocks unlimited workshops, VIP networking dinners, 1-on-1 mentorship sessions, and lifetime recording access.
What perks are included in the $5,999 Team Pass? +
The Team Pass covers up to 10 attendees and includes everything in Pro tier, plus a dedicated team lounge, private mini-steer booking, company logo display on the event page, Day 3 recruitment booth access, and priority support.
How do I book 1-on-1 mentorship with keynote speakers? +
Mentorship bookings with key speakers and industry leaders are managed through the Attendee Portal sent to Pro ($749) and Team ($5,999) ticket holders 2 weeks before the event starts.

Yes! All keynote talks and main track sessions will be recorded. Standard pass holders receive 30-day access, while Pro and Team pass holders get unlimited lifetime access.
Will your company pay via Invoice or Purchase Order (PO)? +
Yes! When selecting the Team Pass ($5,999) or bulk registrations at checkout, choose "Pay via Invoice/PO" to receive an invoice with 30-day payment terms for corporate accounting.
